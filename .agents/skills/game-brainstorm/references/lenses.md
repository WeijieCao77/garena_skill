# Lenses and mutation moves

Pick lenses that differ from each other, one seed per lens. Change the set between rounds.

## Lenses

- **Feeling**: getting better, belonging to a squad, bragging rights, surprise, self-expression, collecting, nostalgia, revenge or comeback, a near miss (差一点就赢) that pulls you into one more match, a safe place to be bad at the game.
- **Moment**: the first 30 seconds after login, the queue wait, the second you die, after a losing streak, the post-match screen, day 7 of an event, coming back after months away, when a friend comes online, while watching an esports match, a local festival, on a bad network or a dying battery.
- **Who else is there**: a duo partner, a squad, strangers, spectators, a rival, a content creator, a friend who stopped playing. Try asymmetric play (one plays, one helps) and async play (leave something for a friend to find).
- **AI role**: pick a role from [AI roles](ai-roles.md) that the other seeds have not used.
- **Inversion**: reward losing; let spectators play; let the opponent help; make the feature last 10 seconds; remove the UI and use voice only; let the player design the content.
- **Steal**: borrow a mechanic from another genre or a non-game app and name the source, for example Duolingo streaks, Spotify Wrapped, BeReal's timed prompt, TikTok duets, Pokémon GO, Wordle's shareable grid, or a fantasy sports draft.
- **Place and culture**: festivals and holidays in the target market, school and campus life, mobile-first and low-end phones, shared devices, internet cafés, local language and memes.
- **What ops would ship**: reactivation, invite-a-friend, an event calendar, an anime or IP collaboration, a fair way to spend, creator and esports tie-ins. Ground the idea in a behavior the team would actually measure, and in what a publisher could run in that market (see [designer view](designer-view.md)).

## Mutation moves

- **Combine** two seeds into one moment.
- **Exaggerate** one property tenfold.
- **Invert** who acts, who wins or who pays.
- **Shrink** the feature into a 30-second loop.
- **Make it social**, or make it async.
- **Move** the feature to a different moment.
- **Give the AI a name, a voice and an opinion.**
- **Make it a ritual**: something players do daily or weekly.
- **Make it shareable**: a card, a clip or a challenge link.
- **Add stakes** (something to lose), or **remove the reward** and check whether the idea is still fun.
