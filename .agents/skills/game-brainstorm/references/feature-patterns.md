# Garena titles and brief types

## Starting notes on titles

Treat these as starting points. Search the web to confirm current modes, events and markets before you rely on them.

- **Free Fire**: mobile battle royale with short matches (about 10 minutes, up to 50 players), built to run on low-end phones. Strong in Southeast Asia and Latin America. Has characters with skills, pets, the Booyah Pass, frequent collaborations and FFWS esports.
- **Arena of Valor**: 5v5 mobile MOBA, popular in Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan. Hero mastery, ranked climbing and team coordination drive play.
- **Call of Duty: Mobile**: Garena publishes it in parts of Southeast Asia. Multiplayer and battle royale modes, loadouts and ranked play.
- **Players in these markets** often play with school or work friends, on shared or mid-range Android phones, over unstable mobile data, and follow creators and esports closely.

## Game feature patterns

Use the row that matches the prompt. Pick the player behavior the prototype should change, then make the benefit and trade-off visible in the flow. Verify title-specific or market-specific facts before using them. Label seeded values as simulated.

| Brief | Mechanic to consider | Edge or trade-off to show | Candidate measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Teach a skill, use it once, then show a visible unlock | Experienced player skips guidance or a new player retries after failure | First meaningful action completion, with time to first match as a guardrail |
| Ranked / matchmaking | Give the player a meaningful choice before queuing or a useful decision after the match | Wait time, fair teams, smurfing or hidden advantages | Ranked matches completed, with queue time or imbalance as a guardrail |
| Social / squads | Match by session goal, language or readiness, then join | No suitable party, party fills, player leaves, privacy or spam | Invite-to-play conversion, with reports or unwanted invites as a guardrail |
| Returning players / live events | Explain the most relevant change and offer one catch-up goal | Missed day, expired event, or value for existing players | Return-to-first-match completion, with perceived fairness as a guardrail |
| Pass / economy | Make free and paid value, progress or odds understandable at the decision | Insufficient balance, duplicate reward, unclear price or competitive advantage | Informed completion of the target action, with complaints or drop-off as a guardrail |
| Esports | Link a viewing moment to an actionable in-game experience | Event has ended, stream is unavailable, or reward was already claimed | Watch-to-play conversion, with disruption to viewing as a guardrail |
| Post-match AI | Ground one coaching suggestion in an observable match statistic | Missing data, uncertainty, or competitive assistance during play | Suggested action applied, with perceived usefulness or fairness as a guardrail |
